- **A question reaches you as a question.** When a session stops to ask you something, the Inbox shows the question, with its choices, instead of "turn ended", and you can answer it right there.
- **Stalls are no longer "finished".** A session that stops without finishing, asking, or leaving anything running to bring it back shows as "Stopped without finishing", with its own sound.
- **Refused actions become an ask.** When auto mode refuses something a session tried, the Inbox shows what it was ("Allow Bash: git push --force?") and why, so you can grant it without hunting for it.
- **Send a stalled turn back once.** A new setting tells a session that is about to stall to finish, ask, or set up a wake before it stops. Off unless you turn it on.

## For agents

- End every turn with `tell_finished`: `done`, or `needs-decision` with the question as `label` and up to 6 short `choices`, then stop; the person answers from the Inbox and the answer is typed in here as theirs.
- A turn that ends any other way (no `tell_finished`, nothing set up to wake you) shows the person “Stopped without finishing”.
- When an action is refused by a permission check, ask with `needs-decision` and say what granting it means.
