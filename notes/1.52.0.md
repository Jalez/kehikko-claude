Kind: feature

- **After a restart, a conversation that was waiting on its own background work carries on too.** Before, only one mid-turn was woken. One whose turn had ended while its background tasks or subagents were still running was left waiting for work the restart had stopped.
- **The wake-up says why.** After an app update it says Kehikot restarted to update, and to which version, then to carry on where it left off. A conversation that was idle, finished or waiting for you is still never woken.
