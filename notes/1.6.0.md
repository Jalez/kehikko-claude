- **New conversations find their place by themselves.** A conversation you start in a Claude container is filed under that kehikko as soon as you send it something, and becomes the kehikko's own when it has none. No need to type /kehikot-here. A conversation you moved somewhere else stays where you put it.
- **Claude knows what is around it.** From its first answer, Claude knows the project, epic and parts it is working on, and which modules are on the kehikko with it, along with their tools and skills. It can use them without looking first.
- **Told when the kehikko changes.** If you add a module to the kehikko or take one off while a conversation is running, Claude hears about it with your next message, and only once.
