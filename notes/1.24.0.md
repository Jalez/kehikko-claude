- **A restart no longer says a background conversation was cut off.** When Kehikot restarts, a conversation running in the background goes on: only its view closed. It is no longer woken with "Kehikot restarted while you were working", and no "resumed after Kehikot restarted" line appears for it.
- **"Needs your attention" goes once you are back in the conversation.** A line about failed or unfinished background work, a question, or a resumed conversation goes when you type in that conversation, wherever you type: in Claude, in Claude Code's own view or in another terminal. It also goes when the conversation starts working again later. A line left up by an earlier restart goes the next time Claude starts, if you have been in the conversation since.
- **Every project is checked.** With more than one project open, lines in projects other than the first no longer stay up after you have moved on.
