- **Messaging works in every conversation that says it can.** A conversation started or resumed from the Claude container now always gets its own connection to this module, so asking it to message another session works. Before, a conversation woken while Kehikot was still starting, or one that was missing that connection, was told it could message but could not find the tools until you reconnected it.
- **Conversations that can't message say so.** A conversation that has no connection of its own to this module is no longer told it can message. It is told how to get messaging instead: open it again from the Claude container.

## For agents

- `send_message` and `read_messages` on kehikko-claude are listed whenever your door carries your place. If your notes say this conversation cannot message, it has no such tools, and searching for them will not find them.
