- **Conversations survive restarts.** A conversation now runs in Claude Code's background, and the container only shows it, so updating Claude or restarting Kehikot no longer stops it, its agents or its background commands. It is there again when the container comes back.
- **Resumed after a restart.** A conversation that was working when a restart cut it off anyway is started again and asked to check what was interrupted, and the Inbox says so. Turn this off with "Resume conversations cut off by a restart" in the View menu.
- **Told when background work was cut off or failed.** When a conversation's background commands or agents didn't finish before the session ended, or failed, the Inbox shows a "needs you" line for it, with what each one was. It goes away once you carry on in that conversation, or dismiss it.
