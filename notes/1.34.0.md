- **Holds every Bash command to the guards of the modules on its kehikko, subagents included.** When a module placed on the kehikko guards a command, Claude runs it the way that module says (for example, through the queue) or refuses it with the module's reason. This applies to the session's own commands and to every subagent's. A module that is not on the kehikko guards nothing. `KEHIKOT_DIRECT=1` in front of a command lets it through when you ask for that.
- **Tells subagents what the modules around them say.** A subagent the session starts is told its place, what each module on the kehikko says to agents, and the guards there. A subagent no longer starts knowing only its brief.
