- **A small Claude is a tile.** In a container too small for a terminal (under about 220 by 160 pixels), Claude shows its mark with a ring that says what the conversation is doing — working, asking you something, needing you, stopped without finishing, finished — and its title. Press it to see the conversation large over the canvas; press Answer to reply to a waiting question with its choices right there. Compact and Clear are there when the tile has room. The conversation keeps running underneath and is not redrawn.
