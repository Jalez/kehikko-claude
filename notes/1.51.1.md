- **A question a session asks you stays in the Inbox until you answer it** (#108). A background task finishing, a Queue report or a Trackers report wakes the session, and that turn used to replace the question with "turn ended". Now the question is an item of its own. It goes when you answer (type in the session, or reply from the Inbox), when the session asks a newer one, or when you dismiss it.
- **The same for an action auto mode refused.** It stays until you answer, not until anything at all is typed into the session.
- **Kehikot's own reports typed into a session no longer count as you.** A cut-off or resumed line now waits for you to say something, not for the next Queue report.
