- **A session reminds itself to say how it ended.** When a session is about to stop without saying it finished, asking you anything or setting up a wake, it is sent back once to tell you where it stands, so the Inbox says "finished" or shows its question instead of "Stopped without finishing". This is now on by default, renamed "Remind the agent to say how its turn ended"; turn it off there.

## For agents

- A turn about to stop without `tell_finished` or a wake set up is sent back once with a reminder: finish, ask with `needs-decision`, or set up what will wake you.
