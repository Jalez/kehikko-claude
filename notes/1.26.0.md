- **Messages from other sessions arrive by themselves.** A message from another agent session is now typed into the conversation it is for, framed as that session's words, so it reads it without being reminded to check its messages.
- **Busy and put-away conversations get them too.** A conversation that is working gets the message as its next prompt, and one that was put away while quiet is resumed with it. A question waiting for your answer is never typed into.
